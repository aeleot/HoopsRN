Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

hoopsRN is an iPhone app for finding pickup basketball games. This page says what the app stores about you, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The short version: we store the account and game information the app needs to work, we do not run ads or analytics, we do not sell your information, and your device's location never leaves your phone.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Your email address and passwordTo sign you in and let you reset your password. Sign-in is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication; we never see your password.
A user IDA random identifier created with your account, used to tie your runs, friends and squads to you.
Your display nameSo other players can recognize you. New accounts start with a generated name such as "Hooper 4821"; you can change it.
Your home court, favorite courts and search radiusTo show you nearby courts and runs. These are courts you pick from the app's list, not your device's location.
Runs you create, join or are waitlisted forTo schedule games and show rosters.
Friends, friend requests, squads, squad invites and season matchesTo run the social and squad features, including the results both squad leaders report.
How many runs you have completed, your weekly streak and when you last playedTo show your stats.
Players you block and reports you sendTo stop unwanted requests and to review content that breaks our rules.

Your location

If you allow it, the app reads your device's location to measure how far away courts and runs are. That location stays on your phone. It is not sent to us and we do not store it. If you decline, the app measures distances from downtown Durham instead.

The map is Apple Maps. Displaying it sends map requests to Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What stays on your phone

Your recently viewed courts, your light or dark appearance setting, the inbox cards you have dismissed, and reminders for upcoming squad matches are kept on your device only. They are cleared when you sign out or delete your account.

What other players can see

  • Your profile is visible to everyone signed in to hoopsRN. That includes your display name, home court, the month you joined, and the other profile details listed above other than your email address. Other players can find you by searching for your display name.
  • Your email address is never shown to other players.
  • Public runs, and who is on them, are visible to everyone signed in. A private run is visible to the people on it and to anyone who has its invite link.
  • Squads, their members and their season match results are visible to everyone signed in.
  • A friendship or friend request is visible only to the two people involved.
  • Your block list is visible only to you. Reports are visible only to the people who run hoopsRN; the player you report is not told who sent it.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who processes your information

hoopsRN stores its data with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), on servers in the United States. Google processes that data on our behalf under Firebase's privacy and security terms. We do not share your information with anyone else, unless the law requires it or it is needed to protect someone's safety.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time: Profile → Delete Account. This removes your profile, sign-in, friends, friend requests, squad invites and block list, calls off runs you host, and takes you off runs and squads you joined. A squad you lead is disbanded.

A few things remain, with your name and email no longer attached to them:

  • Runs that have already finished and squad matches that were already played, so that other players' history stays accurate. A finished run keeps only your random user ID, which no longer points to a profile. A squad match keeps the name of the squad that played it.
  • Reports you sent, which we keep as a moderation record.

If you cannot use the app, email chaseallen122@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, the information described above is removed right away, apart from the items listed as remaining.

Your choices

  • Change your display name, home court and search radius in Profile.
  • Turn location access on or off in iPhone Settings.
  • Turn match reminders on or off in iPhone Settings → Notifications.
  • Block a player, or report a player or squad, from their profile or from a match.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by emailing us.

Children

hoopsRN is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to it is limited by server-side rules that let each account change only its own information. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed without permission.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change is significant, we will also say so in the app.
