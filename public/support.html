Support

Need help with hoopsRN, or want to tell us about a problem? Email chaseallen122@gmail.com. We read every message and aim to reply within two days.

Report a player or a squad

The fastest way is in the app. Open the player's profile, tap the … button, and choose Report. For a squad you are matched against, open the match and tap Report at the bottom. You can also block a player from the same menu. Reports are reviewed, usually within 24 hours, and the player is not told who sent it.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile → Delete Account. It asks for your password and removes your account and data right away. If you cannot get into the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is removed.

Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password? We will email you a reset link.

A court is wrong or missing

Court details come from public map data, and some will be out of date. Email us the court's name and what needs fixing.

Where hoopsRN works

Courts currently cover the Raleigh–Durham area of North Carolina. If you turn location off, the app centers on Durham.
